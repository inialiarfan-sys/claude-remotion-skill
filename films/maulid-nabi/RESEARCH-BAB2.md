# Bab 2 — research and reference checklist

*Perjalanan Hidup Rasulullah SAW*, Chapter 2: **Zaman Remaja dan Kehidupan Sebelum Kenabian**. Each claim shown on screen is listed below with its basis and how it is presented.

The status key follows `RESEARCH-BAB1.md`:

- **Established**: Qur'an, or an authentic hadith with a confirmed reference.
- **Sirah (mainstream)**: Ibn Ishaq as transmitted by Ibn Hisham, and later standard works. Shown with a sirah label.
- **Report**: A narration whose details are discussed by scholars. Shown with a label and a disclaimer.
- **Illustrative**: Visual reconstruction only. Labelled *ilustrasi*.

## Research environment

As in Chapter 1, primary hadith and sirah sites were blocked. Verification used:

- the Uthmani Qur'an text (quranenc.com via `quran-json`), extracted programmatically;
- web-search results indexing hadeethenc.com, surahquran.com, dorar.net and shamela.ws. Search snippets were used only to confirm wording and references, never for a scholarly verdict.

## Depiction rules applied

There is no figure of any person anywhere in the chapter. The Prophet ﷺ is never shown as a face, body, silhouette, shadow or glowing human shape. He is represented only by his name, by places (Makkah, the household courtyard, pastures, the road north) and by objects: a shepherd's staff and water skin, sealed bundles, a balance scale, a manuscript page. Animals (sheep, a goat, camels) are the only living beings shown. No conversation, expression or specific action is invented.

## Deliberately left out

- **The quotation «مَا جَرَّبْنَا عَلَيْكَ إِلَّا صِدْقًا»** (Quraysh at Safa) was considered, but its exact wording and number could not be confirmed in this session, so it is not used.
- **Harb al-Fijar and Hilf al-Fudul** are outside this chapter's storyboard. They need their own sourcing.
- **Marriage to Khadijah, the rebuilding of the Ka'bah, and Hira** belong to later chapters and are not revealed here.
- **Any age** for the Syam journey, the shepherding or the title al-Amin.
